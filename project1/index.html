Female Radicals in Chinese Characters

A collections of the Chinese characters that encode gender bias, particularly the ones related to women.

Introuction (click to hide)

Chinese characters are not abstract letters.They are built from visible parts, each carrying meaning.

The radical 女 means "woman." It is also used as a building block inside many other characters.

The same component repeatedly appears in words with negative meanings which reflect cultural bias. There are many more characters follow the same pattern. Even neutral words related to women have been systematically stigmatized over time.

This site collects the Chinese characters that encode gender bias, particularly the ones related to women. For each character, the site will present: their meanings, historical origin (if available), and possible alternative characters (if available).
